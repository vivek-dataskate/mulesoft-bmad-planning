## What We're Actually Selling

We are not selling integrations. We are selling the ability to operate like a technology company.

Most businesses spend enormous energy moving data manually — exporting spreadsheets, copying records between systems, chasing down reports from vendors. Every hour spent on that is an hour not spent on the business. Every manual step is a place where things go wrong, get delayed, or get dropped.

DataSkate's job is to eliminate that layer entirely — and then go further. Once your systems are connected and your data flows reliably, automation becomes possible. And once automation is possible, AI agents can start taking actions autonomously on your behalf.

That is the journey we guide clients through. Integrations are step one.

---

## The Three Stages We Take Clients Through

### Stage 1 — Connected (Year 1)
Your systems talk to each other. Data moves automatically, in real time or on schedule. Your team stops doing manual data entry, stops running exports, stops being the integration layer between your software.

*For a healthcare GPO:* New members flow from the website into Salesforce automatically. Shopify purchases sync to member records without anyone touching a keyboard. Vendor utilization reports process themselves.

### Stage 2 — Automated (Year 1–2)
Rules and triggers fire without human involvement. A member goes quiet for 90 days — a re-engagement sequence starts automatically. A vendor report arrives — it is parsed, matched, and loaded without Caralina touching her inbox. Mailchimp campaigns are fed by live Salesforce segments, not weekly exports.

### Stage 3 — Agentic (Year 2+)
AI agents monitor your connected systems, detect patterns, and take actions. They surface next-best-action recommendations to your sales team. They flag anomalies in utilization data before your vendors do. They operate your business processes — with your team in the loop for approvals, not for execution.

**This is what the 2-year partnership is for.** Year one gets you to Stage 2. Year two builds toward Stage 3. No other integration partner structures engagements with this arc in mind.

## Objection Handling

### "We just need the integrations — we're not thinking about AI."
> That is exactly where every one of our clients starts. What we do differently is make sure the integrations we build today don't become a problem when you are ready for automation. It is not about doing more — it is about not having to redo things later. The architecture decisions we make in month one determine what is possible in year two.

### "The monthly cost adds up."
> It does — and so does everything it replaces. One integration developer at $100k/year. Support contracts. Incident response at 2am. Time your team spends being the integration layer between your software. When you add that up, $150/flow/month for a full managed service is usually the cheapest option by a significant margin.

### "We want to own the integration."
> You do own it — all the code is in your GitHub repository, you can take it anywhere. What the managed service covers is the operation of it. You own the asset. We run it. If you ever want to bring it in-house, nothing stops you. We just make it unnecessary.

### "2 years is too long."
> The 2 years is the minimum needed to actually get you to Stage 2. Year one, you're getting your systems connected and reliable. Year two, you're starting to automate. A 6-month engagement just delivers flows and leaves. We stay long enough to make sure the investment pays off.

### "What if we want to change a flow?"
> Minor changes — field mappings, configuration, performance tuning — are included. The line is action changes: if the business logic of the flow needs to change fundamentally, that is a new flow conversation. We scope it, price it at the current rate, and add it. It is not a surprise — it is just a new capability.

### "We don't have the internal resources to manage this."
> That is the point of the managed service. You do not manage it. We do. Your only job is to use the data in Salesforce and make business decisions based on it. We handle everything underneath.
