How We See Your Business ▲

AgileMind sells K-12 educational curriculum (print student activity books + digital access) to school districts. Products are sold by academic year with back-to-school seasonality. Print materials carry regional ISBNs that vary by state.

Phase 1
Connected
Agreement-to-Invoice and Inventory in One View
The 4 confirmed flows close the manual gaps Crystal and Holly live in daily: UC1 fires when Abby creates an Agreement in Salesforce (Closed Won) and automatically creates the QuickBooks invoice — no manual re-entry. UC2 polls QuickBooks on a schedule and writes the invoice number, payment date, and open balance back to the Salesforce Agreement — Crystal stops toggling between systems. UC3 reads Holly's Google Sheets or Excel demand worksheet on a schedule and loads inventory quantities, committed stock, and projected shorts into a new Salesforce Inventory__c object. UC4 pulls QB inventory item costs into the same Salesforce object, so Maria can answer 'how much did we spend on Intensified Algebra Vol 1 over 5 years?' without leaving Salesforce.
Phase 2
Automated
Shortage Alerts and Fulfillment Triggers Fire Themselves
With live inventory in Salesforce, automated rules become possible that aren't feasible today. When available inventory for a title drops below the configured 3% buffer (currently tracked manually in red on Holly's spreadsheet), Salesforce fires a shortage alert to Holly and the relevant sales rep automatically. When a district's pipeline stage crosses 60% probability, a pre-fulfillment reservation is triggered in QB — reducing the last-minute crunch at back-to-school. CCR fulfillment flags (UC6-potential) are stamped automatically on the incoming Agreement so the system knows materials were already shipped, eliminating Holly's manual reconciliation call to sales. Product catalog sync (UC5-potential) keeps QB items and Salesforce Products aligned so new title additions by Maria don't silently diverge from Abby's opportunity line items.
Phase 3
Agentic
Agentforce Turns Salesforce Data Into District-Level Decisions
Phase 1 creates the Salesforce objects that Agentforce runs on: Agreement, Invoice status, Inventory__c, and Product. An Agentforce Sales Agent monitors open Agreements where the QB open balance has aged past 30 days and drafts a follow-up email to the district's finance contact — no action needed from Crystal. A second Agentforce agent answers Maria's natural-language procurement questions: 'What did we source for Intensified Algebra Vol 1 the last 3 fiscal years and what is left in inventory?' — pulling from the Inventory__c and Invoice history Phases 1–2 created. A third agent runs pre-season demand forecasting: reading open pipeline by probability stage from Opportunities, comparing against current QB inventory levels, and generating a draft purchase order recommendation for Holly's review — replacing the manual red-cell Excel exercise with a one-click approval workflow.
