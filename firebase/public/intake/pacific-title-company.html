How We See Your Business ▲

Pacific Title processes 150–200 closings a day across approximately 50 escrow officers, and 10–15 minutes of each order is spent re-keying purchase contracts, lender orders, and amendments into ResWare by hand — "the bulk of our time spent," in Lexi's words. This engagement makes that step stop. ResWare lifecycle events flow into Salesforce in real time as Account, Contact, Opportunity, and Title_Order__c records — and an Anypoint IDP layer reads the incoming intake mailbox so contracts pre-populate ResWare without any escrow officer touching a keyboard. Most answers in this form are already filled in from your 2026-04-09 deep-dive call. Your job is to confirm they are correct and fill in the blanks we genuinely couldn't know.

Phase 1
Phase 1 — Connected
ResWare and Salesforce stop being two separate worlds
Order opens in ResWare → Account, Contact, Opportunity, and Title_Order__c records appear in Salesforce, keyed by ResWare orderId. Email arrives at the intake mailbox → Anypoint IDP extracts buyer/seller/property/loan fields → ResWare draft order is created (high confidence) or a Salesforce review task is posted (low confidence). The manual re-keying step Lexi called "the bulk of our time spent" stops on go-live day.
Phase 2
Phase 2 — Automated
The order lifecycle starts moving itself
Encompass becomes the flagship Phase 2 target — title commitment and closing data flows to the high-value lender's LOS through the same MuleSoft API-led abstraction. Campaign Monitor engagement events land on Salesforce activity timelines. Phase 3
Phase 3 — Agentic
Salesforce AgentForce becomes PTC's closing concierge
Phase 1's clean Title_Order__c data is what AgentForce reads in Year 2 — it answers "which open orders are at risk of missing their closing date," "which BDR drove the referral on this funded order and at what cost," and "should we accept this new lender's order given current capacity" without anyone toggling between systems. The Salesforce objects and MuleSoft event triggers established in Year 1 are what AgentForce reads — that architecture is decided once.
