**Authoritative files:**
- Full pricing rules: `commons/sales/pricing-model.md`

- Reference proposal (MRN, 10 flows): `projects/mrn-healthcare/intake/proposal-mrn-healthcare.html`

**Core proposal principle:** All proposals lead with the three-stage AI transformation journey (Connected → Automated → Agentic), not with pricing. Pricing is Section 8 of 10. See `commons/sales/proposal-structure.md`.

**Pricing summary:**
- Standard: $3,500/flow
- New AE (first-ever DataSkate deal): $10,000 flat ≤5 flows; $3,500/flow beyond 5
- IaaS (AE sells full $50k Anypoint license): $0 upfront
- Managed service: $250/flow/month, escalates 5% every 6 months for ALL active flows
- 1-year minimum, no early exit, no discounts, paid upfront every 6 months (2 payments)
- Rate schedule: P1 $250, P2 $262.50

**What's included:** implementation, uptime, performance, notifications, upgrades, minor enhancements (no action changes)
