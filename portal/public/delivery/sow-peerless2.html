Engagement Summary

DataSkate will design, configure, and deliver a MuleSoft-powered integration connecting HD Portal, Salesforce, ComputerEase, DocHub, GCP, and Deltek — to sync Leads, Jobs, Customers, Contracts, Quotes, Appointments, Tasks, and Payments across 12 confirmed flows. DataSkate manages the MuleSoft runtime, error handling, and monitoring as a fully managed service; Peerless Fence does not own or operate the middleware layer.

This SOW is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to conflict-of-law principles. Any dispute shall first be escalated to senior management of both parties.
