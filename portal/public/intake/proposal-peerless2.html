The Challenge
Why this matters now
▼

Every manual handoff between HD Portal, Salesforce, and ComputerEase is a silent failure waiting to happen — and the security exposure underneath it makes it worse. 'There's no firewall. They could put in a username and password and keep trying and trying and trying until they get in.' The goal of Stage 1 is simple: close the exposure, stabilize the data flow, and make sure nothing breaks quietly.

Ashley's Morning Queue

Ashley manually checks the HD Portal backend every morning to find new leads, then re-enters them into Salesforce — a process that delays consultation scheduling and introduces transcription errors on every lead that comes through the Home Depot channel.

Job Entry Is a Hand-Off Chain

When a Salesforce opportunity closes, Ashley manually creates the customer record in ComputerEase, enters deposit amounts and payment terms from the HD Portal contract, and then uploads signed contract documents (299A, 299B, 118) into the ComputerEase job record — each step a separate manual touch with no automated validation.

Security Exposure on the ComputerEase VM

'There's no firewall. They could put in a username and password and keep trying and trying and trying until they get in' — the GCP instance hosting ComputerEase currently allows SSH and RDP from any IP address, meaning the job-costing system is reachable by anyone on the internet.

ComputerEase API Is Not Yet Active

'It's an ancient system... I feel like I've traveled back to the 90s' — ComputerEase API access has not been enabled with Deltek, the CE Live Service relay is not configured, and API version compatibility (v26+) has not been confirmed, blocking every automated flow in this proposal until a support ticket is resolved.

ROI / Business Case
The business case for moving now
▼

Peerless Fence runs 6+ systems — HD Portal, Salesforce, ComputerEase, DocHub, GCP, and Deltek — with no automated handoffs between them. Ashley manually checks HD Portal every morning, re-keys lead data into Salesforce, then re-enters job details into ComputerEase by hand. Every closed deal that touches all three systems is a manual relay race with no error detection until something breaks downstream.
