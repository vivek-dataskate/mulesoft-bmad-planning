The Challenge

Crystal re-keys. Holly builds worksheets. Maria runs QB reports. None of this scales.

AgileMind's revenue runs on multi-year district agreements — but the operational layer connecting Salesforce, QuickBooks, and inventory is 100% manual. During the April–August print procurement window, every data handoff is a spreadsheet, a manual QB report, or a re-keyed entry.

Agreement-to-Invoice Gap

Crystal manually creates QB invoices from finalized Salesforce agreements. One missed agreement or wrong amount is a delayed revenue collection and an audit risk at renewal.

No Payment Visibility in Salesforce

Maria runs manual QB reports to surface payment status. Sales reps have no live view of which agreements are paid, outstanding, or overdue — making renewal conversations guesswork.

Inventory Lives in Spreadsheets

Holly's demand worksheet is the only place anyone can see how many Student Activity Books and Teacher Books are in stock. If the file is out of date, procurement decisions for the back-to-school run are wrong.

No Single Source for ISBN Inventory

Physical title inventory is tracked across QB Items and Holly's spreadsheet — neither is authoritative. Fulfillment decisions for the April–August peak season are made with incomplete data.

The Solution

MuleSoft as your integration hub

MuleSoft connects Salesforce, QuickBooks Online, and your inventory data sources into a single real-time pipeline — eliminating manual re-keying, giving every team member live data, and building the data infrastructure AgileMind needs for AI-powered procurement planning.

UC1 and UC2 are bidirectional — Salesforce writes to QB, QB writes back to Salesforce. All flows monitored 24/7 with automatic retry on failure.
