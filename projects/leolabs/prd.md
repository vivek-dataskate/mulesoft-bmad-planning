**Problem Statement:**
A high-growth aerospace data company (~30 sales reps, 40% YoY growth) manages sales orders, commissions, and financial forecasting manually across Salesforce, NetSuite, and Excel spreadsheets. When a deal closes in Salesforce, Sales Orders must be manually re-entered in NetSuite; invoices and payment statuses are manually exported from NetSuite for finance forecasting; commission tier calculations for 25–30 reps are performed manually each quarter, producing frequent errors and disputes with zero real-time rep visibility. The business consequence: slow order-to-cash cycle, compounding data quality errors as volume grows, and a Sales Ops and Finance function spending multiple days per month on work that should be automated — with increasing risk as the team scales toward 50+ reps.

## Business Requirements

1. When a deal closes in Salesforce, a corresponding NetSuite Sales Order must be created automatically — no manual re-entry.
2. The NetSuite Sales Order ID must be written back to the Salesforce Opportunity immediately after creation (cross-reference requirement — stated as a critical pain point on the scoping call).
3. Products and pricing defined in Salesforce must be available as NetSuite items without manual synchronization.
4. When prices change in Salesforce, the corresponding NetSuite item prices must update automatically.
5. When NetSuite generates an invoice from a Sales Order, the invoice number, due date, and amount must appear in Salesforce for finance cash flow forecasting.
6. When payment status changes in NetSuite, Salesforce must reflect the updated status within one business day.
7. All integration flows must retry automatically on transient failures and alert the operations team if retries are exhausted — no silent data loss.
8. The integration must support phased delivery: product/price sync first, then sales order creation, then invoice and payment sync — to reduce risk and match team capacity.

## Pain Points / Current State

- **Spreadsheet hell:** Commission tiers calculated manually for 25–30 reps across disparate Excel files each quarter — tier-calculation errors lead to disputes and "extreme user frustration" (SPM brief).
- **Data drag:** Data manually exported from NetSuite and Salesforce, merged in Excel to compute splits and margin-based commission kickers.
- **Zero rep visibility:** Commission information delivered via manually prepared quarterly "excerpts" — no self-service view.
- **Order creation bottleneck:** Sales Orders in NetSuite created manually after every closed deal in Salesforce — no automated link between the two systems.
- **Invoice and payment tracking gap:** Finance team manually exports NetSuite invoice and payment data for cash flow forecasting — described as time-consuming and error-prone.
- **Data quality risk:** Acknowledged by the team — duplicate customer names, mismatched addresses, and product/pricing inconsistencies exist between Salesforce and NetSuite today. These must be resolved before go-live or the integration will propagate errors.
- **Accounting team "beefing up" NetSuite:** The Core AE is currently working with the accounting team to improve NetSuite's ability to identify Salesforce-originated deals — field mapping work is in progress and must be coordinated with DataSkate.
