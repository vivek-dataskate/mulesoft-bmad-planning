●Manual "Spreadsheet Hell": Currently calculating 
commissions for 25–30 reps manually across 
disparate spreadsheets.
●The "Data Drag": Manually pulling data from 
Salesforce and NetSuite and merging them in 
Excel.
●Error Prone & High Stress: Manual 
tier-calculation errors leading to extreme user 
frustration.
●Shadow Visibility: Reps have zero real-time 
visibility; manual "excerpts" are a massive time 
sink.

●Self-Service Flexibility: Quickly change tiers and 
accelerators (e.g., 50% margin kicker) without 
support tickets.
●Scalability for Complexity: Support for complex 
splits, mission-based territories, and international 
expansion.
●System Integration: Automated flow between 
Salesforce, NetSuite, and the commission tool.
