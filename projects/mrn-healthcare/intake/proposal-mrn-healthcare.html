Flow 01
WordPress Member Intake Medium
Gravity Forms → Salesforce + Mailchimp
New and returning member form submissions automatically create or update the Salesforce Account + Contact and add the member to Mailchimp — eliminating the manual backlog entirely.
Flow 02
Welcome Email Automation Low
New member event → Mailchimp
Every new member instantly receives a welcome email via Mailchimp. Bypasses the current WordPress SMTP issue — no more silent failures on onboarding.
Flow 03
Shopify Store 1 — Customer Sync High
Shopify Store 1 → Salesforce
Customer creates and updates in Store 1 automatically create or update the corresponding Salesforce Account and Contact — purchases always linked to the right member.
Flow 04
Shopify Store 1 — Order Sync High
Shopify Store 1 → Salesforce
New orders from Store 1 create purchase records in Salesforce linked to the member account — contract utilization tracking that starts the moment an order is placed.
Flow 05
Shopify Store 2 — Customer Sync High
Shopify Store 2 → Salesforce
Same logic as Flow 03, running independently for Store 2. Includes automatic update of the LegitScript certification field in Salesforce when a member completes certification.
Flow 06
Shopify Store 2 — Order Sync High
Shopify Store 2 → Salesforce
New orders from Store 2 create purchase records in Salesforce. Each order is tagged by source store so utilization reports can distinguish Store 1 from Store 2 activity.
Flow 07
Mailchimp Engagement Sync Medium
Mailchimp → Salesforce
Email opens, clicks, and unsubscribes from Mailchimp flow back to Salesforce in real time. Your sales team can see which members are actively engaged — and which have gone cold.
Flow 08
PharmacyHub Utilization Import High
PharmacyHub SFTP → Salesforce
PharmacyHub utilization report files are automatically picked up, parsed, and loaded into Salesforce on your schedule. No more files sitting in email inboxes.
Flow 09
Pipeline Medical Utilization Import High
Pipeline Medical SFTP → Salesforce
Same automated ingestion for Pipeline Medical reports — processed independently from PharmacyHub so a failure in one never affects the other.
Flow 10
Vendor Report API High
Vendor API → Salesforce
A secure REST API endpoint DataSkate exposes for additional utilization vendors to POST data directly. No SFTP required — vendors push data, MuleSoft validates and loads it.
