In 5 years
Private-equity hold periods typically run 5–7 years from initial acquisition (Jan 2023). The integration platform built now becomes an asset in Akoya's eventual exit narrative — architecture decisions affect the multiple, not just operations.

Worth knowing
The Elevate Fence trademark (USPTO 98608324) is registered to "Peerless Group Holdings, L.P." — meaning the Elevate brand is a deliberate platform thesis, not a generic portfolio company. Akoya is treating fence consolidation as a branded roll-up, which changes how integration spend should be approved at the platform level vs. each operating brand.
Why it's not in your research: USPTO trademark records rarely surface in operating-brand strategy discussions; the LP naming reveals the platform thesis only to readers who look for it.

Worth knowing
Five other operating brands sit under Elevate Fence Partners alongside Peerless: Allied Fence, Florida State Fence, Jan Fence, My Fence and Railings 2 Go, and Foremost Fence. None shows a public integration footprint yet. A successful Peerless go-live becomes the reference architecture for the entire portfolio.
