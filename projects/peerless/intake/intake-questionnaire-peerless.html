How We See Your Business ▲

Peerless Fence Group is a residential fencing installation company headquartered in West Chicago, IL, operating across Chicagoland, Illinois, Wisconsin, Indiana, and Ohio. As a Home Depot Pro contractor partner, HD generates leads that Peerless sales reps convert into fence installation jobs. The core operational bottleneck today is manual cross-referencing between three systems — HD Portal, Salesforce, and ComputerEase — for every new order: a process that consumes 45–90 minutes of one operations manager's time per job.

Phase 1
Connected
HD Portal, Salesforce, and ComputerEase talk automatically
Every new HD order triggers automatic field validation, contract download, and job creation in ComputerEase — eliminating the 45–90 minutes Ashley spends manually cross-referencing systems for every new job.
Phase 2
Automated
Compliance and corrections run without human intervention
Mismatch alerts fire in real time as soon as an order arrives. Salesforce corrections sync back to HD Portal automatically. No manual follow-up, no re-entry, no compliance backlog in Ashley's queue.
Phase 3
Agentic
AI monitors job cycles, contract quality, and margin trends
With clean data flowing across all three systems, AI agents can score contract compliance patterns per sales rep, flag at-risk installs before customer complaints arrive, and surface seasonal demand signals from lead volume trends.
