The Challenge

Three systems. Zero automation. One person in the middle.

Peerless processes every new Home Depot order by manually cross-referencing HD Portal, Salesforce, and ComputerEase — three systems that don't talk to each other. Every new job costs 45–90 minutes of Ashley Salerno's time before a single nail is driven.

45–90 Min Per Order

Every new HD order requires Ashley to open HD Portal, open Salesforce, open ComputerEase, and manually verify that all three agree — field by field, contract by contract. There is no automated check.

Broken Lead Sync

The HD Portal → Salesforce lead sync is currently unreliable. Ashley checks HD Portal manually every day to find orders that never appeared in Salesforce. Jobs are missed. Revenue is delayed.

15-Field Manual CE Entry

Every new job requires manually entering 15+ fields into ComputerEase — job number (tracked in a spreadsheet), material codes, territory codes, commission amounts, product type, and more. No validation, no automation.

No Compliance Guardrails

SKU mismatches, missing signatures, and contract discrepancies between systems are caught manually — or not at all. Compliance issues surface after the fact, when they are hardest to fix.

MuleSoft as your integration hub

DataSkate deploys MuleSoft as the connectivity layer between HD Portal, Salesforce, and ComputerEase. Every new order is validated, processed, and filed automatically — without Ashley opening a single system manually.

Proposed Integrations — 7 Flows

All 7 included at one flat price — complexity never raises your invoice

ⓘ
UC3 (ComputerEase job creation) is the most complex flow in this engagement
ComputerEase's API routes through Deltek's CE Live Service relay — a non-standard connectivity topology unique to legacy Deltek products. This is fully scoped and understood. It does not raise the price, but it does require pre-development setup (see Key Assumptions). DataSkate has planned for this architecture from day one.
Flow 01 — UC1
HD Portal → Salesforce — Order Sync & Validation High
HD Portal → Salesforce
New HD orders are automatically synced to Salesforce as Opportunities. Replaces the broken manual sync Ashley checks daily. Every order is validated against Salesforce data on arrival — field mismatches flagged immediately.
Flow 02 — UC4
SKU & Material Code Validation Medium
HD Portal + Salesforce → Validation layer
Material codes and SKUs on every incoming order are automatically cross-checked against the approved product catalog before the order advances. Mismatches halt the order and alert the ops team — not the customer.
Flow 03 — UC5
Legal Compliance & Signature Check Medium
HD Portal contracts → Compliance validation
Every 299A and 299B is automatically checked for signature legibility and required field completeness. Contracts that fail compliance are flagged before they reach ComputerEase — catching issues at the source, not the install.
Flow 04 — UC2
Salesforce → HD Portal Correction Write-Back High
Salesforce → HD Portal
When corrections are made in Salesforce, they automatically sync back to HD Portal. Eliminates the manual re-entry loop — no more keeping two systems manually in sync when a field changes on the Salesforce side.
Flow 05 — UC3
Salesforce → ComputerEase Job & Customer Creation High
Salesforce (Closed Won) → ComputerEase
When an Opportunity closes as Won in Salesforce, MuleSoft automatically creates the job record in ComputerEase — all 15 required fields populated from mapped data. Job number generated and tracked via MuleSoft Object Store. Customer record created if new.
Flow 06 — UC6
HD Portal Contract Download Medium
HD Portal → Salesforce
299A, 299B, and 118 waiver contracts are automatically downloaded from HD Portal and attached to the corresponding Salesforce Opportunity. No more logging into HD Portal to pull contracts manually — they appear in Salesforce on their own.
Flow 07 — UC7
Change Order Processing High
HD Portal (PDF) → Salesforce + ComputerEase
Change orders arrive as PDF documents in HD Portal. MuleSoft extracts the structured data, creates change order records in Salesforce, and updates the corresponding job in ComputerEase. No manual re-entry when a customer changes their order.
