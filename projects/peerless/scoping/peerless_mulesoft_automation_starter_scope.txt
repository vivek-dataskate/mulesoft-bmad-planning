Scope

Under the terms outlined in this SOW and the Agreement, DATASKATE will furnish the Professional Services detailed below. This section's scope encompasses MuleSoft setup, implementation of business flows, and consultation-based services ("Expert Services") provided by DATASKATE consultants specializing in specific subject areas and the services offered will be remote [DataSkate Office]. The scope of implementation is limited to MuleSoft Automation Starter.

DATASKATE will deliver Implementation Services to the Customer in the below use case.

Automated cross-system field comparison & mismatch detection

Source Systems: HD Portal, Salesforce, Deltek, and ComputerEase

Target System: Cross-system validation layer/Salesforce (where new orders trigger the workflow)

Requirement: The system must pull data from all four core systems, compare required validation fields (such as customer info, materials, and pricing), and execute real-time mismatch detection to flag only material discrepancies for human review

Auto-sync of Salesforce corrections back to HD Portal

Source System: Salesforce

Target System: HD Portal

Requirement: Because sales reps update customer and quote data in Salesforce, the integration must automatically sync these corrections back to the HD Portal to resolve the current one-way data flow and eliminate recurring discrepancies

Automated job creation in ComputerEase

Source System: Salesforce / HD Portal

Target System: ComputerEase

Integration Type: Real-time (triggered when a new HD order appears)

Requirement: The integration must automatically create a new customer record and a new job in the accounting system, syncing financial data, commission calculations, and overhead structures to eliminate manual data entry

Promotion SKU validation logic

Source System: HD Portal and Salesforce

Target System: Cross-system validation layer

Integration Type: Real-time

Requirement: The system must automatically validate promotion SKUs and discount allocations across systems to ensure they match the contract, eliminating the operational risk of human errors negatively impacting margins

Legal compliance checks

Source System: HD Portal (the contract system of record)

Target System: Cross-system validation layer

Integration Type: Real-time

Requirement: The automation must verify required legal compliance fields, specifically checking the 3-day right of rescission date and ensuring signatures are present and legible

Automated contract download and attachment

Source System: HD Portal

Target System: Salesforce

Integration Type: Real-time (triggered when a new HD order appears)

Requirement: Because contracts currently do not sync, the integration must automatically download the signed contracts (299A/B) from the HD Portal and upload/attach them to the proper records in Salesforce

Change Order Processing

Source System: Email/Google Drive

Integration Type: Real-time or Near Realtime depending on Source System

Requirement: Process the Change Order received in PDF format to extract data update back in the system
