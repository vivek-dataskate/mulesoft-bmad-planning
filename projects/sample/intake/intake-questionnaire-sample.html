How We See Your Business ▲

Acme Corp is a mid-market specialty retailer with 280 locations across 14 states, running Salesforce for CRM and NetSuite for ERP. The sales team closes deals in Salesforce, but order fulfillment, inventory visibility, and invoicing all live in NetSuite — creating a data gap that forces ops to manually reconcile both systems daily.

Phase 1
Connected
Order-to-Fulfillment Without Manual Handoffs
Closed Salesforce opportunities trigger order creation in NetSuite automatically. Inventory availability and shipment status flow back into Salesforce so reps stop calling ops for updates.
Phase 2
Automated
Demand-Driven Inventory Alerts
With live pipeline data and inventory in one place, automated alerts fire when open opportunity demand exceeds available stock — giving procurement a 3-week lead time instead of reactive scrambling.
Phase 3
Agentic
AI-Powered Reorder Optimization
An AI agent reads closed-won velocity, current stock levels, and supplier lead times to recommend optimal reorder quantities per SKU — reducing both overstock carrying costs and stockout-driven lost sales.
