Proposed Integrations
3 Proposed Integration Flows
▼
Flow 1
Deal-to-Subscription
HubSpot → MuleSoft → NetSuite
When a deal moves to Closed-Won, MuleSoft creates the NetSuite customer, subscription, and first invoice automatically — eliminating the 48-hour manual relay and letting provisioning begin in minutes.
Flow 2
Invoice & Payment Sync
NetSuite → MuleSoft → HubSpot
Invoice payment status and renewal dates sync back to HubSpot deal records in real time. Account managers see billing health without running NetSuite reports — overdue accounts surface before the renewal call, not after.
Flow 3
Revenue Alerts
NetSuite → MuleSoft → Slack
Key billing events — subscription created, invoice overdue, renewal approaching — fire automated Slack messages to the right channels. Ops and revenue teams are notified without anyone monitoring a dashboard.
