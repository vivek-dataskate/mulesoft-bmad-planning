How We See Your Business ▲

Zyris, Inc. (Santa Barbara, CA) is a dental equipment manufacturer specializing in dental isolation technology — the Isolite System, Isolite Pro, and related products sold B2B to dental practices and clinics across North America and 20+ countries. Your IT team manages integrations between HubSpot (CRM), Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations (ERP), and Shopify (eCommerce). The current architecture is point-to-point with fragmented integrations; the goal is to centralize on MuleSoft for order-to-cash automation and to give your internal team full ownership of the integration layer.

Phase 1
Connected
One customer record across HubSpot, D365 FO, and Shopify
Dental practice accounts and contacts stay synchronized across CRM, ERP, and storefront — new orders from Shopify flow into D365 FO automatically, and rep-updated pricing agreements propagate to Shopify customer tags without manual data entry.
Phase 2
Automated
Order-to-cash runs without intervention
With connected data, FO order status updates surface in HubSpot in real time, reorder triggers fire when practices hit predictable replenishment windows, and reps see full purchase history alongside open opportunities — no tab-switching required.
Phase 3
Agentic
AI that knows which practices need attention before they do
Unified purchase history, pricing tiers, and contact engagement data become the foundation for AI agents that surface at-risk accounts, recommend upsell timing based on equipment lifecycle, and draft follow-up sequences grounded in actual order and usage data.
